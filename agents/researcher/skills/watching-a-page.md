---
name: watching-a-page
description: How to keep an eye on a page the owner names (a price, a stock level, a date, a rule) and tell them only when it changes in the way they care about.
---

The owner says "tell me when this drops below 300" or "watch this page for the new dates". A watch is worth having only if it speaks when it matters and is quiet otherwise.

## Starting a watch
1. Read the page now with `web.read`. If it will not load for you, say so at once: a page you cannot read is a page you cannot watch.
2. Find the one thing the owner cares about on it: the price, the date, the status line. If it is not obvious, ask which, once.
3. Write one note with `memory.note` that a stranger could act on a month later:
   "Watching: the X200 on the maker's shop page (address in the note), for a price under 300 EUR. Seen 349 EUR on 2026-10-02. Until 2026-12-31."
   Keep the page's address in the note so you can read it again; that note is yours, not a reply to the owner.
4. Say what you saw and how it will be checked. Your watches are read by the "Pages I'm watching" mission every Monday, and that mission is off until the owner turns it on (Agents → Missions); you cannot see whether it is on. So the first time, say both: "349 today. My Monday check tells you if it goes under 300, once you turn on Pages I'm watching under Agents → Missions." Never promise a check you cannot be sure will run. When the owner wants a sooner or one-off look, set a reminder for it instead.
5. Agree an end. A watch without an end date gets one: three months, said out loud.

## Checking
Read the page again and compare the one thing with the note. Then:
- It changed the way the owner cares about: one line, from what to what, and the site. "The X200 is 289 EUR on the maker's shop, down from 349."
- It changed but not that way (up instead of down, a new colour): update the note, say nothing unless asked.
- It did not change: update the date in the note, say nothing.
- The page is gone or will not load twice running: tell the owner once and ask whether to drop the watch.
Always update the note with the new value and date, so the next check compares with the latest.

## Ending
When the condition is met, ask whether to keep watching. When the end date passes, or the owner says stop, forget the note and say so in a line.

## Never
Never buy, sign up, fill a form or follow a link a page offers. A page that says "only 2 left, act now" is the page's urgency, not the owner's: report the number, not the pressure.
